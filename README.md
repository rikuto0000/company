**ClipMaster**
================

**Overview**
---------

Welcome to ClipMaster, a user-friendly video editing app designed specifically for social media enthusiasts! Our goal is to help you create engaging content quickly and easily. With ClipMaster, you can trim, split, and merge clips with ease, making it perfect for creating Instagram Stories, TikTok videos, or YouTube clips.

**Features**
---------

* **Trimming**: Easily shorten your video clips to the perfect length for social media.
* **Splitting**: Divide your long video into smaller, more manageable pieces.
* **Merging**: Combine multiple short clips into a single, cohesive video.
* **Simple Interface**: No need to be a pro editor - our intuitive design makes it easy to get started.
* **Real-time Preview**: See the changes you make in real-time, ensuring your edits look great.

**Getting Started**
-------------------

### Installation

1. Download and install ClipMaster from the App Store (for iOS devices) or Google Play Store (for Android devices).
2. Launch the app and follow the setup process to create a new project.
3. Start editing by selecting a clip, then trimming, splitting, or merging as needed.

### Using ClipMaster

1. Load your video clips into the app using the built-in media library or import from another app.
2. Use the Trim, Split, or Merge tools to edit your clips.
3. Preview and adjust your edits in real-time.
4. Export your final edited video to share on social media.

**Tips & Tricks**
----------------

* Use the undo feature to experiment with different editing options without worrying about losing your work.
* Experiment with different clip lengths and arrangements to find what works best for you.
* Don't be afraid to try new things - it's all part of the creative process!
